## Is my CV stored anywhere?

Not on our servers. It stays in your browser. When you press an AI button the text is sent to produce that one result and then discarded. Turn on "Save my workspace on this device" if you want it to survive a reload; clear the site's data to remove it.

## What is the Evidence Score?

The share of the advert's requirements your CV already evidences, weighting must-haves fully and nice-to-haves half. Every requirement is backed by a quoted line from the advert and, where found, a quoted line from your CV. It is a coverage measure, not an ATS score or a hiring prediction.

## What does the Gap Interview do?

For a requirement your CV does not mention, it asks two yes/no questions. If you have the experience, it drafts a bullet from your answer and places it under the right role. If you don't, it says so and offers a truthful "learning" line instead. It cannot invent experience for you.

## What is free?

Three role matches a month, with the full requirement list and what is missing. Pro and Premium add suggestions you can apply, the Gap Interview, the CV studio and templates, interview questions and answer review. Premium adds follow-up questions, timed mock sessions and saved CV versions. Plans are in the app under the plan chip.

## Can I cancel?

Any time, from Plan & billing → Manage. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Where do the job listings come from?

Public job boards (Adzuna, Arbeitnow). We send them only your search words and location, never your CV. Apply through the original advert.

## Which AI does Roledone use?

An AI agent run on a major model provider, instructed to quote your CV for every claim and blocked from adding numbers or tools you did not write. Anything it cannot support is shown as a question for you instead. See the Privacy Policy for how text is handled.
