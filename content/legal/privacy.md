## The short version

- Your CV and the job adverts you paste are processed to give you a result. They are **not stored on our servers**. They live in your browser, and only there, unless you choose to save your workspace on your device.
- When you press an AI button, the text you are working on is sent to our AI provider to produce that result, and then discarded. Nothing is sent until you press the button.
- We keep what we need to run your account and subscription: your email address, your plan, and counts of how many AI calls you have used. We never store the content of those calls.
- We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your CV to train AI models.

## What we collect and why

**Account details.** When you sign in with a magic link or Google, our authentication provider (Supabase) creates an account record holding your email address, a user id, sign-in times, and, for Google sign-in, the name and profile picture Google shares. We use this to recognise you across devices and attach your subscription to you. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

**Subscription and billing.** Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us which plan you bought, its status and renewal date, and a customer reference, and we store those alongside your account. Stripe's own privacy policy governs what Stripe holds. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations (tax and accounting records).

**CV, job adverts, answers and drafts.** Everything you type or upload is kept in your browser's storage. It is sent to our server only when you ask for an AI result, is forwarded to our AI provider to produce that result, and is not written to disk or any database by us. If you turn on "Save my workspace on this device", it is also saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a reload; clearing site data removes it. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

**AI processing.** Our AI provider is Anthropic. We send only the text needed for the feature you pressed, with instructions that it may not invent claims about you. Anthropic processes that text to return a result and, under the terms we use the service on, does not use it to train models. Where the provider offers caching to reduce cost, cached fragments of the prompt expire within minutes.

**Usage metering.** To enforce plan allowances and understand costs we record, per AI call: the feature used, the model, token counts, latency, estimated cost, your plan, and an identifier (your account id if you are signed in, otherwise a random id stored in your browser, otherwise a hashed network address). We never record the content of the call. Legal basis: legitimate interests (running and protecting the service) and performance of a contract (plan limits).

**Job search.** When you search for roles, the words you type and the location and country you choose are sent to our job data providers (Adzuna, Arbeitnow). Your CV is never sent to them.

**Technical logs.** Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (network addresses, request paths, timings) for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB) for: your sign-in session, your plan preview and device id, your workspace if you chose to save it, and your uploaded Word document so your own design can be exported. We do not set tracking cookies. Our payment and sign-in providers set their own cookies on their own domains during checkout and sign-in.

## Who we share data with

Only the processors needed to run the service: Supabase (authentication and database, EU/UK or US regions as configured), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI processing), Adzuna and Arbeitnow (job search words only), and our hosting provider. Each acts under a contract that restricts what they may do with the data. We share personal data with no one else unless the law requires it.

## International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or the provider's adequacy-based arrangements, so that your data receives equivalent protection.

## How long we keep things

- Account and subscription records: while your account exists, then up to 6 years for records we must keep for tax and accounting.
- Usage metering rows: 13 months, then aggregated or deleted.
- Content you send for AI processing: not retained by us; transient at the AI provider.
- Browser storage: until you clear it or sign out.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we will verify it is you and respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

Deleting your workspace is in your hands: clear this site's data in your browser. Deleting your account: email us and we remove the account and subscription record (Stripe keeps the payment records it is legally required to keep).

## Children

Roledone is for people seeking work and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
