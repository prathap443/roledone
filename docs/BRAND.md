# Roledone — working identity

## Meaning

A career is built from many threads: projects, decisions, skills, and outcomes. Roledone connects them into a coherent story for the next role. Pronunciation: “role-woh-vuhn”.

Tagline: **Real experience. A clearer story.**

## Design system

- Ink `#192c26`, ivory `#f7f8f2`, lime `#d8f36a`, sage `#eaf0dd`.
- Manrope headings, DM Sans interface, restrained Georgia italic accents.
- Arrow mark conveys a next step; orbital lines connect experience to opportunity.
- Spacious desktop side navigation; five-item mobile navigation at the bottom.
- Source excerpts and plain explanations instead of opaque score gauges.
- Clear empty states instead of fabricated customer activity or testimonials.
- Calm, specific, encouraging copy. No guarantees or rejection diagnosis.
