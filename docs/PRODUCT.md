# Product brief

## Audience and promise

Initial hypothesis: UK data engineers applying for mid-level and senior roles want help explaining relevant experience and preparing to discuss it. Promise: turn a CV and one job description into a clearer, truthful application and a focused practice session.

## Primary journey

1. Import or paste a CV; confirm extracted text in the future parser flow.
2. Paste a job description; extract requirements with source spans.
3. Compare each requirement against candidate-provided evidence.
4. Review a proposed CV edit with original wording and rationale; confirm every change.
5. Practise five questions grounded in the role and candidate experience.
6. Record the application outcome without implying causation.

## Prototype acceptance criteria

- The sample journey is usable at desktop and phone widths.
- Each detected skill has an exact source excerpt or an explicit missing-mention state.
- Original CV text remains available during editing.
- CV export reflects the user's current draft.
- Interview answers survive navigation within the tab.
- Applications can be added and statuses updated.
- Persistence is opt-in and data can be exported/cleared.
- No AI capability, score calibration, or market traction is invented.

## First commercial experiment

Test a £29 application pack with 10–15 potential users. This is an unvalidated price hypothesis. Offer one role-specific CV review plus interview preparation; initially deliver with human review. Do not collect payment in this prototype.

Interview users about their latest real application, current tools, time spent, prior purchases, and reasons to trust or reject suggestions. Ask them to complete the journey using a redacted CV. Measure task completion, which changes they accept, what they find incorrect, and whether they will pay for the next application pack. Define go/no-go thresholds before recruitment; avoid treating compliments as purchase intent.

## Delivery sequence

1. Test this interactive prototype; adjust audience, workflow, and offer.
2. Add authenticated storage and secure PDF/DOCX text extraction with user confirmation.
3. Add structured server-side AI analysis and evidence-linked proposed edits.
4. Add approved CV rendering/export and account deletion.
5. Add usage metering and payments after willingness-to-pay testing.
6. Collect explicitly consented application outcomes; keep descriptive analysis distinct from causal claims.

## Deferred

Voice interviews, salary prediction, employer ATS emulation, hidden recruiter budgets, career twins, market forecasts, and cross-user outcome models. No assumed six-week production guarantee.
