# Roledone launch video — plan

**What it is:** Roledone compares your CV to a job advert, quotes the lines that prove each requirement, and turns what's missing into new CV bullets and interview practice — from your real experience only.
**Who for:** tech professionals applying for roles. **Sets it apart:** every claim is quoted from your own CV; the AI is blocked from inventing experience.
**Visual hook:** a job requirement appears, and the exact line from the CV that proves it slides under it with "Evidence found". Then one that has no line — "Gap" — and the product turns it into a bullet.
**Tone:** polished. Dark green (#13241d → #223c32), paper (#f7f8f2), lime (#d8f36a). Manrope display, DM Sans body — the app's own identity and real CSS classes.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 s. Music 120 BPM (beat = 0.5 s); every cut on a beat.
**Data:** the app's fictional sample (Alex Morgan, Northstar Analytics). No real names, emails or keys.
